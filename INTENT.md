# SURVIVOR — what the owner wants

Read this before changing anything. It is the owner's intent, written down because it is hard to say in a message.

## The target
**Don't lose positions easily.** Grow the balance steadily over time instead of sitting at what was deposited.
A loss is acceptable. The same loss happening again for the same reason is not.

## How it should behave
1. **Run all the time.** Live, real money, no floor, no daily cap. Never stop because of a limit — keep gathering experience.
2. **Enter early, don't chase.** Take a side when the move is small and early. A move that already happened is priced in.
3. **Don't panic out.** No selling in the first seconds on noise or an empty order book. Only sell at a real price.
4. **Take the profit when it's there.** Sell near the top (bid 0.90) rather than hoping for the last cent.
5. **Learn from every trade.** Each result gets a reason. A pattern that keeps losing becomes a rule that skips it.
6. **One bet per side per window.** Never pile several coins onto the same tick.

## How to change it
- **Fix what is broken; don't tune for activity.** Most losses this far came from bugs, not from the strategy.
- **Live results beat backtests.** Three times a backtest promised a win rate the live market didn't deliver.
- **Judge by money, not by win rate.** Return per $1 staked. An 80% win rate can still lose money.
- **Need a real sample before a rule.** 40+ trades to trust a pattern; 15+ only to be more careful.
- **Change one thing at a time, and say why with numbers first.**
- **Keep it simple.** No new indicators because they sound clever.

## What the owner has seen, in their words
- Positions sold within seconds of opening, then the market went the predicted way.
- Positions reached a solid profit, weren't sold, then turned into a loss.
- Telegram said one thing, Polymarket showed another.
Each of these turned out to point at something real. Take the next one seriously too.
